# SVNEPAI Architectural Clarification

This file records a project clarification for use in future work concerning SVNEPAI. It supplements the repository's existing documentation; it does not modify or replace the published manuscript, and it must not be presented as an experimentally validated implementation.

## Core architectural understanding

SVNEPAI means **Sovereign Virtual-Native Embodied Personal AI**. The Automated Phone is not simply a smartphone containing an AI assistant or chatbot. It is an architectural vision in which a personal AI agent is integrated into the phone's architecture as its embodied personal intelligence.

The agent is conceived as a continuing personal entity across tasks, AI models, and devices, rather than a collection of independent assistants or temporary application-based interactions. The architecture connects:

- A virtual representation of the owner.
- Personal identity and continuity.
- Embodiment, potentially including a face and voice.
- Capabilities provided through applications and direct system functions.
- Governed memory, permissions, and user-controlled interaction.

## Sovereignty principles and distinctions

- Local-first does not necessarily mean offline-only.
- Capability is not permission, and permission is not autonomy.
- Human authority must govern identity, memory, permissions, connectivity, and auditing.
- Observe when authorized; use when necessary; remember when permitted.
- Refusal is a valid system outcome.

## Architectural integrity

Do not reduce SVNEPAI to a conventional AI assistant, a chatbot integrated into a phone, a standalone mobile application, a cloud-dependent personal assistant, or a simple collection of AI tools.

Do not invent architectural components or claim proposed mechanisms have been implemented or experimentally validated. Do not silently modify the original published manuscript or present recommendations as approved architectural decisions.

In analyses, reviews, and discussions, clearly distinguish:

1. Documented architectural facts.
2. Technical interpretations and inferences.
3. Proposed extensions or recommendations.
4. Unresolved questions and unknowns.
5. Decisions explicitly approved by the architecture's creator.

Treat this clarification as a reference for evaluating whether interpretations accurately represent SVNEPAI. Repository documentation or other accessible project sources should be used to distinguish what is documented from what this clarification adds.
